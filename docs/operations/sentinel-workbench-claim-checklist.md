# Sentinel Workbench claim checklist

Public result language requires an eligible independent corpus, frozen truth,
complete B0–B3 controls, valid `k=3`, matched B3−B2 re-score, calibration and
power lineage, and a digest-bound independent reproduction record.

Do not claim effectiveness from CMC inventory, CMC transport, missing truth,
instrument-invalid, not-measured, or underpowered/descriptive states.
